1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & SEPARATION OF CONCERNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project is built adhering strictly to the software engineering principle of 
Separation of Concerns (SoC). Code is partitioned into dedicated, single-purpose 
modules rather than bundled into a monolithic file:

Directory Layout:
plantCatalogue/
├── index.html                   # Semantic HTML5 markup (UI structure, skeleton)
├── css/
│   └── styles.css               # Dedicated presentation stylesheet (design system,
│                                # variables, responsive grid, animations, modal)
├── js/
│   ├── data.js                  # Data model: 24 botanical species profiles with
│   │                            # 10 horticultural fields & 43 photo mappings
│   └── app.js                   # Application controller: reactive search filter,
│                                # category chips, carousels, accordion drawers,
│                                # fullscreen lightbox modal, and keyboard events
├── Images/                      # Complete field photography archive (43 JPEG files)
│   ├── 20261011_142425.jpg
│   ├── 20261011_142431.jpg
│   ├── ...
│   └── 20261011_150138.jpg
└── plant_catalogue_documentation.txt  # Comprehensive botanical & technical companion

Architectural Advantages:
- Maintainability: Plant records can be added, updated, or audited in `js/data.js`
  without touching UI logic or HTML markup.
- Design Flexibility: Styling modifications in `css/styles.css` take effect across
  the entire application without risk of breaking DOM handlers.
- Performance & Caching: Browsers cache external CSS and JS independently, reducing
  re-download overhead on subsequent visits.
- Scalability: Easy migration path to REST APIs or headless CMS in the future.

3. COMPREHENSIVE BOTANICAL PROFILES & HORTICULTURAL CARE DIRECTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each profile includes all required growing parameters:
- Colour of Foliage
- Flower Characteristics
- Watering Cycle
- Optimal Sun Exposure
- Suitable Weather & Environment (Indoor vs Outdoor)
- Nutritional Requirements
- Soil Requirements
- Wind Conditions
- Cultivation, Propagation & Care Tips

================================================================================
PROFILE 01: FLAMINGO FLOWER / TAIL FLOWER (Anthurium andraeanum)
================================================================================
- Family: Araceae (Arum Family)
- Associated Image: Images/20261011_142425.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Glossy deep emerald green, leathery heart-shaped (cordate) leaves
  with pale prominent venation.
- Flower Characteristics: Waxy scarlet-red, blush-pink, or pure white cordate spathes
  featuring an upright cream-to-yellow flower spadix; individual blooms last 6-8 weeks.
- Watering Cycle: Moderate: Water when top 1-2 inches of potting mix feel dry; prefers
  consistent slight moisture; requires 60%+ relative humidity; never let roots sit in water.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Medium to bright indirect filtered sunlight; avoid harsh direct
  sun which causes spathe bleaching and foliar sunburn.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Indoor & Sheltered Patio; warm tropical/subtropical
  (18°C - 30°C / 65°F - 86°F); highly sensitive to chilling below 15°C (59°F).
- Nutritional Requirements: High-phosphorus liquid fertilizer (NPK 10-20-10) diluted to
  1/4 strength every 4-6 weeks during active growth; top-dress with organic vermicompost.
- Soil Requirements: Coarse, ultra-aerated, free-draining aroid mix (pH 5.5 - 6.5):
  40% coco chips/coir, 30% perlite, 20% orchid bark, and 10% vermicompost.
- Wind Conditions: Sheltered: Intolerant of strong air currents, cold drafts, and AC vents.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Epiphytic perennial clumping habit. Wipe leaves with a damp cloth
  to maximize photosynthesis. Contains calcium oxalate; keep away from pets.

================================================================================
PROFILE 02: POINSETTIA / MEXICAN FLAME LEAF (Euphorbia pulcherrima)
================================================================================
- Family: Euphorbiaceae (Spurge Family)
- Associated Image: Images/20261011_142431.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Dark forest green lobed leaves beneath flamboyant terminal whorls
  of modified bracts in crimson-red, salmon-pink, or creamy-ivory.
- Flower Characteristics: Small, button-like yellow cyathia clustered in the center;
  the showy 'petals' are actually brightly colored botanical bracts.
- Watering Cycle: Thoroughly soak when the top inch of soil is dry to touch; drain pot
  completely; waterlogged soil leads quickly to pythium root rot.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Bright indirect light indoors; 6-8 hours of gentle filtered light
  daily; avoid scorching direct sunlight through glass.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Indoor & Filtered Courtyard; 16°C - 24°C (60°F - 75°F);
  drops foliage if subjected to sudden temperature drops below 12°C.
- Nutritional Requirements: Balanced liquid feed (NPK 20-20-20) at half strength every
  3-4 weeks in spring/summer; suspend feeding during peak winter bract display.
- Soil Requirements: Porous, fast-draining potting soil (pH 5.8 - 6.5): 50% coco peat,
  30% sterilized soil/sand, 20% perlite.
- Wind Conditions: Sheltered: Susceptible to leaf and bract abscission if placed in
  drafty doorways or exposed to exterior wind gusts.
- Growth Habit & Tips: To induce winter blooming, provide 14 hours of continuous total
  darkness every night for 8-10 weeks starting in early autumn. Milky latex sap may irritate skin.

================================================================================
PROFILE 03: GARDEN CROTON (Codiaeum variegatum cultivars)
================================================================================
- Family: Euphorbiaceae (Spurge Family)
- Associated Images: Images/20261011_143203.jpg, 144445.jpg, 144454.jpg, 144502.jpg,
  144509.jpg, 144517.jpg, 144526.jpg, 144537.jpg, 144639.jpg, 144709.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Vibrant multi-chromatic tapestry of fiery scarlet, golden-yellow,
  orange, magenta, peach, bronze, and dark emerald; varies by cultivar:
  * 'Petra': Broad oval leaves with luminous red, orange, and yellow veins.
  * 'Mammy': Corkscrew-spiraled leaves blushing from chartreuse to deep crimson.
  * 'Gold Dust': Forest green elliptical leaves freckled with golden stars.
  * 'Gold Finger': Narrow finger-like blades splashed with gold.
  * 'Picasso's Paintbrush': Thread-like ribbons of red, yellow, and green.
- Flower Characteristics: Slender racemes of tiny star-like cream blossoms; insignificant
  compared to the flamboyant foliage.
- Watering Cycle: Moderate to high: Maintain evenly moist soil during hot weather; water
  when top 1/2 inch is dry; do not allow complete root desiccation.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Bright direct to intense indirect sunlight; needs 4-6 hours of
  sunlight daily to synthesize and maintain rich anthocyanin foliage pigmentation.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Indoor Sunny Windows & Outdoor Tropical Patios;
  20°C - 32°C (68°F - 90°F); sheds leaves rapidly below 15°C (60°F).
- Nutritional Requirements: High-potassium liquid or slow-release fertilizer (NPK 14-14-14)
  monthly in spring and summer; top-dress with cow manure or vermicompost.
- Soil Requirements: Rich, loamy, organically enriched potting substrate (pH 6.0 - 6.5):
  40% garden soil, 30% coco peat, 20% vermicompost, 10% perlite.
- Wind Conditions: Moderate tolerance once established; avoid cold, dry gusty winds.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Bushy woody shrub. Wipe leaves to prevent spider mites in dry air.

================================================================================
PROFILE 04: CHINA DOLL / EMERALD TREE (Radermachera sinica 'Golden')
================================================================================
- Family: Bignoniaceae (Trumpet Vine Family)
- Associated Image: Images/20261011_143359.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Delicate, bipinnate, feathery leaves with an ultra-glossy sheen,
  glowing in lime-green and bright chartreuse-gold.
- Flower Characteristics: Fragrant, trumpet-shaped yellow/white blossoms in large panicles
  (rare on indoor specimens; typical on mature outdoor trees).
- Watering Cycle: Moderate: Keep uniformly moist without soaking; water when top inch
  feels dry; resents drought and waterlogging equally.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Bright indirect sunlight; thrives near east-facing windows; gentle
  morning sun deepens the golden foliage hue.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Indoor Bright Rooms & Shaded Verandas; 18°C - 26°C;
  protect from frost and dry heating drafts.
- Nutritional Requirements: Balanced houseplant fertilizer (NPK 10-10-10) at half strength
  monthly from March through September.
- Soil Requirements: Fertile, spongy, well-aerated mix (pH 6.0 - 7.0): 50% coco peat,
  30% leaf compost, 20% perlite.
- Wind Conditions: Sheltered: Highly sensitive to drafts; sudden changes in location or
  drafty airflow induce leaf drop.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Fast-growing. Prune growing tips frequently to encourage bushy branching.

PROFILE 05: DWARF GLOBE THUJA & NEW GUINEA IMPATIENS (Thuja occidentalis & Impatiens)
================================================================================
- Family: Cupressaceae / Balsaminaceae
- Associated Images: Images/20261011_144023.jpg, 144040.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Scale-like aromatic conifer foliage in golden-emerald paired with
  bronze-green fleshy succulent impatiens leaves.
- Flower Characteristics: Impatiens produce vibrant 5-petaled flowers in brilliant scarlet,
  coral, magenta, and white with spurred nectaries; continuous summer-to-autumn bloom.
- Watering Cycle: Thuja is moderately drought-tolerant; Impatiens need consistent, generous
  moisture and must be watered every 1-2 days during warm weather.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Thuja thrives in full to partial sun; Impatiens prefer morning sun
  with dappled afternoon shade.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Outdoor Gardens, Landscape Borders & Large Planters;
  Thuja is cold hardy; Impatiens thrive in 18°C - 28°C and are frost tender.
- Nutritional Requirements: Balanced slow-release pellets (NPK 10-10-10) in spring for Thuja;
  high-potassium liquid feed every 2 weeks for prolific impatiens flowering.
- Soil Requirements: Rich, moist, organically amended soil (pH 6.0 - 7.0): 40% garden soil,
  30% organic compost/cow manure, 30% coco peat/sand.
- Wind Conditions: Thuja has high wind tolerance; tender succulent impatiens stems need
  protection from buffeting gales.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Classic companion planting combining architectural evergreen
  symmetry with continuous floriferous color.

PROFILE 06: POTHOS 'N'JOY' / PEARLS & JADE (Epipremnum aureum 'N'Joy')
================================================================================
- Family: Araceae (Arum Family)
- Associated Image: Images/20261011_144906.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Compact ovate-cordate leaves with crisp glacier-white blotches
  sharply contrasted with deep emerald green centers.
- Flower Characteristics: Extremely rare in cultivation; grown for variegated foliage.
- Watering Cycle: Low to moderate: Allow the top 50% of the soil to dry between waterings;
  tolerant of slight underwatering.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Medium to bright indirect light; requires ample indirect light to
  sustain crisp white variegation without burning.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Indoor & Sheltered Balcony; 18°C - 28°C (65°F - 82°F);
  keep above 12°C in winter.
- Nutritional Requirements: Balanced liquid feed diluted to 1/4 strength every 4-6 weeks
  in spring/summer.
- Soil Requirements: Porous, breathable mix (pH 6.1 - 6.8): 40% coco peat, 30% perlite,
  20% orchid bark, 10% vermicompost.
- Wind Conditions: Sheltered indoor air; avoid harsh AC currents.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Slow-growing compact vine; perfect for hanging pots or small desks.

PROFILE 07: WAX BEGONIA / CLUBBED BEGONIA (Begonia x semperflorens-cultorum)
================================================================================
- Family: Begoniaceae (Begonia Family)
- Associated Image: Images/20261011_144921.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Glossy, rounded, succulent leaves in bright emerald-green with
  subtle bronze-crimson margins.
- Flower Characteristics: Continuous sprays of 4-petaled blossoms in cherry-red, blush-pink,
  and pure white centered with bright yellow stamens.
- Watering Cycle: Moderate: Water when the top 1/2 inch of soil dries; avoid wetting foliage
  directly to prevent fungal leaf spot.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Bright indirect light to dappled shade; tolerates gentle morning sun;
  harsh midday sun scorches succulent leaves.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Indoor Bright Sills & Outdoor Shady Beds; 18°C - 26°C;
  tender perennial grown as an annual in frost zones.
- Nutritional Requirements: Water-soluble bloom booster (NPK 10-15-10) at half strength
  every 2-3 weeks while flowering.
- Soil Requirements: Light, humus-rich mix (pH 5.5 - 6.5): 40% coco peat, 30% perlite,
  30% aged organic compost.
- Wind Conditions: Sheltered: Juicy, brittle stems snap easily in strong gusts.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Pinch off spent flower clusters to stimulate continuous flowering.

PROFILE 08: DWARF MONDO GRASS & RIBBON GRASS (Ophiopogon japonicus & Liriope)
================================================================================
- Family: Asparagaceae (Asparagus Family)
- Associated Image: Images/20261011_144941.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Dense fountain-like tufts of fine dark obsidian-green blades alongside
  variegated white-striped ribbon grass.
- Flower Characteristics: Small bell-shaped pale lilac/white flowers nestled low among the
  blades in summer, followed by brilliant metallic-blue berries.
- Watering Cycle: Low to moderate: Drought-tolerant once established; water weekly during
  dry spells.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Partial to full shade; tolerates morning sun in temperate areas.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Outdoor Groundcover, Edging & Shaded Courtyards; hardy
  from -5°C to 38°C; thrives in humid tropical gardens.
- Nutritional Requirements: Low feeder: Top-dress with vermicompost or cow manure once in
  early spring.
- Soil Requirements: Adaptable, preferring moist, well-draining organic loam (pH 5.5 - 7.0).
- Wind Conditions: High tolerance: Fibrous, flexible blades withstand heavy winds and foot traffic.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Superb zero-mow turf replacement and rock garden border.

PROFILE 09: VARIEGATED SPIDER PLANT (Chlorophytum comosum 'Variegatum')
================================================================================
- Family: Asparagaceae (Asparagus Family)
- Associated Image: Images/20261011_144950.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Arching linear ribbons with a creamy-white or ivory central stripe
  flanked by vibrant emerald borders.
- Flower Characteristics: Small white star-like flowers on long, wiry, arching stolons,
  which develop miniature plantlets (spiderettes).
- Watering Cycle: Moderate: Allow top 50% of soil to dry; fleshy tuberous roots store water;
  avoid waterlogging.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Medium to bright indirect light; direct hot sun bleaches foliage.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Indoor & Shaded Veranda; 15°C - 30°C (59°F - 86°F);
  keep above 7°C.
- Nutritional Requirements: Balanced liquid feed (NPK 10-10-10) diluted to half strength
  monthly in spring/summer.
- Soil Requirements: Well-draining potting soil (pH 6.0 - 7.0): 50% coco peat, 30% perlite,
  20% vermicompost.
- Wind Conditions: Sheltered to moderate: Flexible arching leaves handle moderate air movement.
- Growth Habit & Tips: NASA clean air plant. Propagate by rooting spiderettes in water or soil.

PROFILE 10: DWARF UMBRELLA TREE (Schefflera arboricola 'Variegata' & Green)
================================================================================
- Family: Araliaceae (Ginseng Family)
- Associated Images: Images/20261011_145001.jpg, 145032.jpg, 145034.jpg, 145044.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Palmately compound umbrella whorls (7-9 leaflets) in solid glossy
  emerald or splashed with butter-yellow and cream.
- Flower Characteristics: Small greenish-yellow flowers in terminal umbels outdoors,
  followed by round orange-to-black berries.
- Watering Cycle: Moderate: Allow top 1/3 of soil to dry between waterings; avoid soggy
  roots; tolerates occasional neglect.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Bright indirect to medium filtered light; variegated forms need
  more light to keep golden patterns; tolerates morning sun.
- Suitable Weather & Environment: Indoor Bright Rooms & Shaded Gardens; 16°C - 30°C;
  protect from freezing.
- Nutritional Requirements: Balanced houseplant fertilizer (NPK 20-20-20) at half strength
  every 4-6 weeks in spring/summer; top-dress with vermicompost.
- Soil Requirements: Fast-draining rich medium (pH 6.0 - 6.5): 40% coco peat, 30% perlite,
  20% organic compost, 10% neem cake.
- Wind Conditions: Moderate tolerance: Sturdy woody stems withstand moderate patio breezes.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Can be pruned to compact shrub, grown as standard tree, or trained
  into dramatic bonsai.

================================================================================
PROFILE 11: GOLDEN NET BUSH & PURPLE ERANTHEMUM (Pseuderanthemum carruthersii)
================================================================================
- Family: Acanthaceae (Acanthus Family)
- Associated Images: Images/20261011_145152.jpg, 145206.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: var. reticulatum has bright lime leaves with a luminous golden-yellow
  network of veins; var. atropurpureum features metallic deep eggplant-purple and bronze leaves.
- Flower Characteristics: Small salverform white tubular flowers with magenta spots in the
  throat, borne on erect spikes.
- Watering Cycle: Regular: Keep soil evenly moist during hot months; water when top inch
  dries; avoid waterlogged roots.
- Optimal Sun Exposure: Morning sun to bright dappled shade; requires ample light to sustain
  rich purple and golden venation.

PROFILE 20: PEACE LILY / WHITE SAILS (Spathiphyllum wallisii)
================================================================================
- Family: Araceae (Arum Family)
- Associated Images: Images/20261011_145729.jpg, 145826.jpg

- Soil Requirements: Moisture-retentive yet well-draining soil (pH 5.8 - 6.5): 50% coco peat,
  30% perlite, 20% vermicompost.
- Wind Conditions: Sheltered: Intolerant of dry AC or heating drafts; needs stable humidity.
- Growth Habit & Tips: Premier NASA air-cleansing plant. Use filtered or rested tap water
  if leaves show brown tips from municipal fluoride/chlorine.

PROFILE 24: HORTICULTURAL SUBSTRATES & ORGANIC AMENDMENTS (Nursery Soil Guide)
================================================================================
- Field Reference Images: Images/20261011_150032.jpg, 143826.jpg
- Substrates Documented on Nursery Reference Boards:
  1. Sterilized Coco Peat (25 Kg / 5 Kg): Pathogen-free coconut coir providing superior
     water retention (up to 8x weight) and root aeration.
  2. Ready Pot Mix: Pre-blended soil formulated with porous mineral aggregates and organic matter.
  3. Vermi Compost Organic Manure: Earthworm castings rich in bio-available NPK, humic acids,
     and beneficial microbial flora.
  4. Cow & Sheep Manure: Composted farmyard manure providing slow-release macronutrients.
  5. Neem Cake (Organic Fertilizer & Nematocide): Cold-pressed neem seed residue that
     repels soil-borne pests (root grubs, termites, nematodes) while inhibiting nitrification.
- Recommended Universal Potting Recipe for Tropical Indoor/Balcony Plants:
  * 40% Sterilized Coco Peat
  * 25% Ready Pot Mix / Loamy Soil
  * 20% Vermi Compost
  * 10% Perlite or Pumice
  * 5% Neem Cake Powder

PROFILE 25: GARDEN COSMOS & CANOPY LANDSCAPE (Cosmos bipinnatus & Beaucarnea)

- Family: Asteraceae / Asparagaceae
- Associated Image: Images/20261011_150138.jpg
- Colour of Foliage: Delicate, feathery, thread-like lacy foliage on Cosmos; pendulous

  buffeting gale winds.

  stores water in its bulbous base.
